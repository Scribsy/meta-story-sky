# Demo video: script (3:00 or less, voice-over, no on-camera recording)

All voices are ElevenLabs:
- **The narrator** ("George") speaks for Scribsy.
- **The Muse** ("Story Sky Muse", designed for this demo) is Story Sky's own voice, as heard in the app.
- **The writer** ("Jessica") tells a story written for the demo: Pip, a tiny hedgehog who keeps a lighthouse.

**The footage is a real live run.** The writer's clips were transcribed by Muse Voice Transcribe, read by Muse Spark
and asked back by the Muse; then Muse Image painted the sky. The run is saved as the app's recorded run
(`public/replay/`), so "Play a recorded run" replays it exactly. Record that in Chrome at 1920×1080 with the page's
audio, and lay each writer clip under the moment its transcript appears.

**The conversation:**
- **MUSE:** "What's the first thing… you see?"
- **WRITER:** "A tiny hedgehog named Pip, keeping a lighthouse out on Thimble Isle. Every night she lights the lamp
  for Mabel."
- **MUSE:** "And what kind of hedgehog is that hedgehog?"
- **WRITER:** "A very old one, in a striped scarf and little round spectacles. She's kept the light for a hundred
  winters, because the lantern only glows when someone tells it a secret."
- **MUSE:** "And when someone tells it a secret, what happens?"
- **WRITER:** "The lantern drinks it up and glows gold, and the secret drifts out over the sea for Mabel. Mabel is a
  whale who sings back to her, far out past the fog."
- **MUSE:** "And where could that whale come from?"
- **WRITER:** "She swam up out of an old sea chart, I think, the kind with dragons drawn in the corners. And every
  morning there's a teacup on Pip's doorstep, still warm. I don't know yet who leaves it."
