# Demo video: script (3:00)

**Steven's time: about 20 minutes.** 5 to read this, 5 for one practice run, 10 to record (two takes).

**Before recording**
- Start the live server with the key in its environment (`META_MODEL_API_KEY=... node server.mjs`), open http://localhost:5177 in Chrome, full screen (F11),
  zoom 110%.
- Record the screen and your mic with **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar), or Clipchamp's screen recorder.
- Have your story idea in your head, not on paper: about 45 seconds, with at least two names, one place, one object
  and one thing you don't know yet. Make it a fresh idea, not a manuscript.
- If anything fails on the take, the page's **Play a recorded run** button is the fallback (say "here's a recorded
  run" and carry on).

| Time | On screen | You say (roughly; your own words are better) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | The first screen: "Tell me about the story you keep coming back to." | "I'm Steven, founder of Scribsy, a writing app for novelists. Every novel starts as a mess in someone's head. This is Story Sky, built this week for the Meta hackathon on Meta's Muse models. It's a standalone demo; nothing in the Scribsy app changes." |
| 0:15–1:05 | Tap **Tap to talk**. Speak your idea (about 45 s). Tap to stop. | (Your idea, out loud. Messy is fine.) |

| 1:20–1:55 | Cards fly in. Hover one card so its words light up in the transcript. Point at the green proof line and the counts. Open **Show what the check threw away** if there is anything. | "Every card quotes me exactly. The page checks each quote character by character against my transcript, and shows where it is. If Muse Spark paraphrases me, the card is thrown away, and you can see which and why. And it doesn't guess about names: a name only becomes a person or a place if I said something that shows it. Otherwise it's just a Name, and I decide." |
| 1:55–2:20 | The **One question** box. Type a short answer and press Enter. | "Then one question, made only from my words: a standard Clean Language question; the model just picks which words it's about. It never answers. My answer joins my words, and a new name in it becomes a card." |
| 2:20–2:35 | Tap three or four cards, then **Keep these →** | "I keep the ones that are mine." |
| 2:35–2:55 | The sky paints in; stars and filaments appear. | "And Muse Image paints my story's sky, with the cards I kept as its constellation. That's the first minute of a book, in my own words." |
| 2:55–3:00 | Hold on the sky; the footer shows the three models. | "Story Sky, by Scribsy. Built on Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Spark and Muse Image." |

**Rules for the take**
- Call it Scribsy or Story Sky, and describe it as a writing app, not a developer tool.
- Say plainly that it runs on Meta's models and that it was built during the hackathon.
- Keep it under 3:00. If the reveal is slow, cut the wait in Clipchamp rather than talking over it.
- Upload as **Unlisted** on YouTube (or a public Loom) and check the link in a private window before submitting.
